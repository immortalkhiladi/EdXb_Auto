EDGAR Deck Converter

Turns a slide deck into an EDGAR-ready package: one HTML 3.2 document with a JPG per slide at 1,050 px width, searchable slide text placed in the background, and GoFiler-style page breaks between slides. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Background text is written in white 1 pt type beneath each image so the filing is full-text searchable while only the slide image is visible.
