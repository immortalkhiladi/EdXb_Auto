Formatting

“Paragraphs”: lines stacked without spacing in Word (statement titles, addresses) stay together.

Financial tables

Page break

Where page 1, the F-pages (F-1 …) and Part II (II-1 …) start and end is found automatically (Word sections, the table of contents, the index to the financial statements, the PART II heading) and can be set by Word page for each document in step 3, Page breaks → Page numbering.

Word’s own page numbers are always removed: PAGE / NUMPAGES fields in headers, footers and text, and typed lines such as “- 3 -”, “Page 3” or “F-3” at a page break (plain “3” or “ii” only when they run in sequence page after page). Headings, numbered paragraphs, (i) / (a) clauses and table values are never touched; anything unconfirmed stays as text and is listed in the report.

The cover is never numbered. The contents pages are i, ii …; page 1 is the page that starts with PART I, Item 1 or PROSPECTUS SUMMARY (or the heading typed above). “Cover page numbering” only applies when numbering follows Word.

Priority: 1 explicit Word break → 2 previous filing → 3 filing section → 4 cover / TOC / signatures → 5 financial statements → 6 Word page boundary → 7 automatic. A lower rule never overrides a higher one; no break is placed inside a heading + text, a title + table, a statement, a note or a signature block.

Table of contents

The link sits at the top of each page, inside the GoFiler page break, and jumps back to the table of contents. “As in Word” keeps it only where Word has a “Table of Contents” line at the top of the page; Off (the default) writes no link and drops those Word lines too; the table of contents itself is unaffected. Single pages can be switched on or off in the HTML view.

YES keeps / links the Word TOC and lets you edit entries, levels and links in Structure review → Table of contents (or build one from the headings). NO writes the Word contents as plain text without links. Column widths must total 100%.

Auto-numbering

% (content 95%)

Word automatic numbers become static numbers in a 5% column with the item text beside them in the 95% column; continuation lines stay in the content column and nested levels are indented.

Standard: every bullet is the GoFiler row [0.25 in][0.25 in ●][text] — the bullet 0.25 in from the left edge, the text at 0.5 in and inside its cell — for Word bullets, typed bullets and bullet tables alike, whatever their Word indent; a nested level of a list goes 0.25 in further in. Bullets inside a table cell take the same positions from the cell edge (EDGAR accepts no table inside a table). “Their Word position” and “Indent levels” keep the earlier placements.

Document model

Page-break sources found

Every distinct Word indent is ranked into a level. Change a level to move a whole group of paragraphs; “keep” writes the original Word value. Output values follow Indent 1 / 2 / 3 from the configuration.

Edit the entry text, its level (TOC 1 / 2 / 3) and the heading it links to; reorder or remove entries. Page numbers are filled from the final page breaks when the HTML is generated. “Rebuild from headings” creates the entries from the document headings for the selected levels; without a TOC in Word it adds one after the cover page.

Spaces inside table cells, numbers and intended line breaks are kept. Blank lines on the cover page are kept; blank spacer rows in tables are always kept.

Page numbering

Where each numbering starts and ends, by Word page (the page count from the top of the Word file, as in Word’s status bar “Page N of M”). Empty boxes keep the automatic value shown in grey. A range ends where the next one starts unless you give it an end; pages outside every range (cover, prospectus back cover, pages after an end) get a page break without a number.
