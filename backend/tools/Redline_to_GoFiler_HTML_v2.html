Redline to GoFiler HTML

Applies the Track Changes of one or more client Word redlines to the filed EDGAR HTML, paragraph to paragraph and table to table; several Word files are applied one after another. Everything outside the revised paragraphs and table cells stays byte-identical; GoFiler’s <R> revision tag can be added, left alone or removed. The Source HTML and the Updated HTML can then be read side by side, with every deletion and insertion marked. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
